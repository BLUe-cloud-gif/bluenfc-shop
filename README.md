# BLUe 

Build me a clean, sophisticated and modern website for BLUe using the uploaded BLUe logo as the official logo.

BLUe sells NFC digital business cards, NFC business card bracelets, NFC review cards, and other NFC products.

DESIGN

* Use the uploaded BLUe logo exactly as provided.

* Clean, premium and professional look.

* White/light background with a subtle touch of blue throughout the website.

* Use blue for buttons, small accents, borders and highlights — not too much blue.

* Modern typography and plenty of whitespace.

* Simple, polished and easy to navigate.

* Mobile-first and fully responsive.

* Subtle animations only.

HOMEPAGE

Create only these sections:

1. Hero

    “Your Business. One Tap Away.”

    Short description explaining BLUe’s NFC products.

    Buttons: Get Started and Explore Products

2. Products

* NFC Business Card Bracelet

* NFC Digital Business Card

* NFC Review Card

3. How It Works

    Tap → Connect → Grow

4. Why BLUe

* No app required

* Instant sharing

* Easy to update

* Professional and reusable

5. Businesses We Serve

    Real Estate, Restaurants, Salons, Spas, Contractors, Professionals, Retail and Small Businesses.

6. Final CTA

    “Turn every introduction into a connection.”

    Button: Get Started

7. Footer

    Logo, Products, How It Works, Contact, Privacy Policy and Terms.

IMPORTANT

Keep this as a simple MVP landing website.

Do NOT build a dashboard, database, payment system, CRM, AI features, complex animations, or unnecessary pages.

Do not over-engineer anything.

Use lightweight components and placeholder product visuals where needed.

Focus on getting a beautiful, professional, fast website finished quickly while using as few Lovable credits as possible.

Do not make additional changes outside this request.
